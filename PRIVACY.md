# Rune AI — Privacy Policy

Rune AI is a student project (Agentic AI course) that creates and manages advertising campaigns on Meta platforms on behalf of the businesses that use it.

**Data we access.** With the advertiser's permission, Rune AI uses the Meta Marketing API to create campaigns, ad sets, creatives and ads in the advertiser's ad account, and reads ad performance metrics (spend, impressions, clicks, conversions) to optimize those campaigns. It reads the name and ID of the Facebook Page the advertiser selects to run ads from.

**Data we do not access.** Rune AI does not collect personal data about people who see the ads, does not read private messages, and does not sell or share any data with third parties.

**Storage.** Campaign settings and performance metrics are stored only in the advertiser's own Rune AI installation. Access tokens are stored locally and never published.

**Deletion.** To delete your data, remove the Rune AI installation or contact the maintainers via https://github.com/ShlokBarot1/Rune-AI/issues. Revoke Rune AI's access at any time in Facebook Settings → Business integrations.
